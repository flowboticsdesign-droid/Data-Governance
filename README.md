# Data Governance – Power BI Dashboard

> A 3-page Power BI report providing full visibility into data governance 
> across compliance, quality, and executive metrics — covering 5,500 records 
> across 6 business domains (HR, IT, Finance, Marketing, Sales, Operations) 
> with GDPR relevance tracking and audit year filtering.

---

## 📋 Pages

### 1. Executive Overview
![Executive Overview](Screenshot/Executive-Overview.png)

> High-level command center for governance leadership. Tracks 25,298 findings, 
> 16,633 open issues, 1,146 critical issues, and a quality score of 93.67. 
> Includes geo-map of open issues by country, compliance status breakdown 
> (Non-Compliant 71.25%), open issues trend over 2024–2025, and drill-down 
> by business domain and data steward.

---

### 2. Data Quality
![Data Quality](Screenshot/Data-Quality.png)

> Operational quality scorecard with avg accuracy 92.8%, completeness 93.9%, 
> consistency rate 88.65%, and duplicate rate 5.84%. Features duplicate rate 
> ranking by data owner, trend analysis by month, classification-level 
> breakdown (Sensitive, Internal, Public, Confidential), and a full quality 
> metrics matrix by business domain.

---

### 3. Data Compliance
![Data Compliance](Screenshot/Data-Compliance.png)

> Financial risk and regulatory compliance view exposing €2.06bn total 
> financial exposure, €1,779.99M estimated fines, and €277M remediation cost. 
> Breaks down fines by compliance risk rating (Critical: €660M), business 
> impact level, and domain — with audit pass rate by frequency 
> (Monthly: 80.37%) and records by audit rating.

---

## 🔍 Key Features

- 3-tab navigation: Executive Overview → Data Quality → Data Compliance
- Dynamic KPI selector (Findings / Open Issues / Critical Issues / Accuracy / Duplicate Rate / Fines)
- GDPR relevance filter and audit year slicer (2024 / 2025)
- Access level breakdown: Read / Write / Admin
- Drill-down by Data Steward and Business Domain
